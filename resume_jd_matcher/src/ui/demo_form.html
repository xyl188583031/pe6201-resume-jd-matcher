Prototype - not for live sites. Simulated form. Nothing is submitted, and nothing is stored. Values are AI suggestions taken from synthetic data; click a field to review a suggestion, then click Fill to accept it.

Application form

Candidates are asked to complete every required field.
How this demo behaves

Each field carries its own confidence. A field below the abstention threshold is withheld and shows a warning instead of a value - one uncertain field does not blank the rest of the form. Nothing is written into the form until you click Fill, which is the human-in-the-loop requirement from section 8 of the Problem Statement.
